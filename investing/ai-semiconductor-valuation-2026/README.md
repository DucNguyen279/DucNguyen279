## Entry-price method

The expanded section calculates a practical buy zone from a conservative or normalized EPS base multiplied by a justified P/E. It applies a lower multiple to cyclical, low-margin, or financing-dependent profits. Current scores weight moat 35%, earnings quality 25%, growth visibility 20%, valuation at the current quote 10%, and balance-sheet resilience 10%.

The buy levels are screening thresholds, not guaranteed fair values. The dashboard marks where company-specific TAM/SAM is not disclosed and uses revenue, GMV, subscriptions, or loan-network volume as distinct SOM proxies rather than conflating them.
